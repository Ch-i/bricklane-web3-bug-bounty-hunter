# NMC-11 | Uint Comparisons

_Section severity (from Solodit section header): Low_  
_Audit firm: Guardian Audits_  
_Source report: [2022-10-02-NFTR.md](https://github.com/solodit/solodit_content/blob/main/reports/Guardian%20Audits/2022-10-02-NFTR.md)_

---

**Description**

When dealing with unsigned integer types, comparisons with `!= 0` are cheaper than with `> 0`.

**Recommendation**

Replace the `assigners[msg.sender] > 0` check with `assigners[msg.sender] != 0`.

**Resolution**

NFTR Team:

- The suggested changes were implemented.
