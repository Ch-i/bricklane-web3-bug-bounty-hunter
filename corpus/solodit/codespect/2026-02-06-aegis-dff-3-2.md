# [I-03] Redemptions from V2 pools temporarily blocked when AegisEngine pool utilization is high

_Section severity (from Solodit section header): Informational_  
_Audit firm: CODESPECT_  
_Source report: [2026-02-06-Aegis-DFF.md](https://github.com/solodit/solodit_content/blob/main/reports/CODESPECT/2026-02-06-Aegis-DFF.md)_

---

**Files:** [`AegisDFFRedeemLib.sol`](https://github.com/labs-solo/AEGIS_DFF/blob/e8d5ed8a07a09a6d36d0c1401e66957540d90093/contracts/libraries/AegisDFFRedeemLib.sol#L266-L296)

**Description:**

The `_withdrawFromPool(...)` function redeems the vault’s proportional AegisEngine shares during user redemptions from V2 pools. It calculates the shares to redeem and calls `AE_MODIFY_LIQUIDITY` with a negative delta via the AegisRouter. Inside the AegisEngine, `modifyLiquidity(...)` converts the shares to a liquidity delta and calls `POOL_MANAGER.modifyLiquidity(...)` to remove full-range liquidity. However, if the pool has high utilization (borrowers have consumed most of the available liquidity), the Uniswap V4 PoolManager will revert because the requested liquidity removal exceeds what is physically available in the full-range position. The AegisEngine does not perform a pre-check against available (non-borrowed) liquidity before attempting the removal.

**Impact:** When AegisEngine pool utilisation is high, all AegisDFF redemption paths will revert for V2 pools. Users are temporarily unable to withdraw their funds until borrowers repay or utilisation decreases. The utilisation cap of 95% means up to 95% of the pool’s liquidity can be borrowed, leaving only 5% available for lender withdrawals.

**Recommendation:** Document this as expected behaviour and inform users that V2 pool redemptions may be temporarily delayed during high utilisation periods.

**Status:** Acknowledged

**Client response:** This is known and can be adjusted in Aegis Engine if needed.
