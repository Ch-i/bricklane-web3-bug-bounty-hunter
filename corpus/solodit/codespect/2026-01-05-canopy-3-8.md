# [I-09] Redundant discriminator space allocation in platform::initialize(...) instruction for platform_collection account

_Section severity (from Solodit section header): Informational_  
_Audit firm: CODESPECT_  
_Source report: [2026-01-05-Canopy.md](https://github.com/solodit/solodit_content/blob/main/reports/CODESPECT/2026-01-05-Canopy.md)_

---

**Files:** [`initialize.rs`](https://github.com/canopyfi/canopy/blob/eaeca1685cb6430890d9d3168b6abb92a7404325/programs/canopy/src/instructions/platform/initialize.rs#L46)

**Description:**

In the `platform::initialize(...)` instruction, the `PlatformCollection` account is initialized with extra space allocated for the account discriminator. The `space` parameter explicitly adds an additional 8 bytes, while the `PlatformCollection::SPACE` constant already includes these 8 bytes as part of its size calculation. This results in redundant space allocation when creating the account, leading to an over-provisioned account size.

```rust
/// Platform collection account for NFT receipts
#[account(
    init,
    payer = authority,
    space = 8 + PlatformCollection::SPACE,
    seeds = [b"platform_collection"],
    bump
)]
pub platform_collection: Account<'info, PlatformCollection>,
```

**Impact:** This issue has no functional or security impact on the protocol. It only results in a small and unnecessary allocation of 8 extra bytes, slightly increasing account size and rent requirements without providing any benefit.

**Recommendation:** Remove the additional 8-byte discriminator from the `space` parameter or adjust the `PlatformCollection::SPACE` constant to exclude it, ensuring the discriminator is counted exactly once.

**Status:** Fixed
