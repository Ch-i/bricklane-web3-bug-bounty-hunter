# [M-02] Liquidity recalculation in withdrawAllEngineShares(...) causes DOS for dismantlePositions(...) when price per share exceeds 1

_Section severity (from Solodit section header): Medium_  
_Audit firm: CODESPECT_  
_Source report: [2026-02-06-Aegis-DFF.md](https://github.com/solodit/solodit_content/blob/main/reports/CODESPECT/2026-02-06-Aegis-DFF.md)_

---

**Files:** [`AegisDFFPositionLib.sol`](https://github.com/labs-solo/AEGIS_DFF/blob/e8d5ed8a07a09a6d36d0c1401e66957540d90093/contracts/libraries/AegisDFFPositionLib.sol#L802-L812)

**Description:**

The `withdrawAllEngineShares(...)` function is invoked during `dismantlePositions(...)` to remove all engine shares from a V2 pool and redeem the underlying value. However, while the function converts vault shares into *liquidity*, the Aegis Engine expects *shares* to be provided during withdrawal. This logic works correctly only when the price per share equals WAD (i.e., `1e18`). If the share price increases due to accumulated fees or interest, the recalculation of redeemable liquidity can exceed the actual share balance, causing the withdrawal to revert.

```solidity
function withdrawAllEngineShares(...) internal {
    uint256 vaultShares = engine.balanceOf(address(this), shareId);
    // ...
    uint256 totalShares = AegisStateLibrary.getTotalSupply(engine, shareId);
    uint256 equityLWad = AegisStateLibrary.getMarketEquity(engine, pid);

    // @audit-issue This recalculation leads to a revert when price per share > 1
    uint256 liquidityToRedeem =
        FullMath.mulDiv(vaultShares, equityLWad, totalShares * 1e18);
}
```

As a result, the computed `liquidityToRedeem` may exceed the amount implicitly represented by `vaultShares`, triggering a revert inside the Aegis Engine.

**Impact:** The `dismantlePositions(...)` function becomes permanently DoS’d for pools where the engine has accrued value beyond the initial deposit. This prevents the owner from successfully closing V2 (engine-backed) pools.

**Recommendation:** Avoid converting shares into liquidity during withdrawal. Instead, pass and redeem `vaultShares` directly, as this is the unit expected by the Aegis Engine and remains consistent regardless of share price appreciation.

**Status:** Fixed
