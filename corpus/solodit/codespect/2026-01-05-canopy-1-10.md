# [M-11] carry_fee may be lost

_Section severity (from Solodit section header): Medium_  
_Audit firm: CODESPECT_  
_Source report: [2026-01-05-Canopy.md](https://github.com/solodit/solodit_content/blob/main/reports/CODESPECT/2026-01-05-Canopy.md)_

---

**Files:** [`execute_airdrop.rs`](https://github.com/canopyfi/canopy/blob/a26f759987b60d503781485eb1743b8cd5cc1463/programs/canopy/src/instructions/tge/execute_airdrop.rs#L154)

**Description:**

In the `execute_airdrop(...)` instruction, the target address for `carry_fee` is not checked.

```rust
/// Platform carry fee token account
#[account(
    mut,
    constraint = platform_carry_token_account.mint == token_mint.key()
)]
pub platform_carry_token_account: Box<Account<'info, TokenAccount>>,

/// Grove carry fee token account
#[account(
    mut,
    constraint = grove_carry_token_account.mint == token_mint.key()
)]
pub grove_carry_token_account: Box<Account<'info, TokenAccount>>,
```

The caller can send platform fees to any specified `TokenAccount`.

**Impact:** The `carry_fee` could be stolen.

**Recommendation:** It is recommended to implement a check for the `platform_carry_account` and `grove_carry_token_account`.

**Status:** Fixed
