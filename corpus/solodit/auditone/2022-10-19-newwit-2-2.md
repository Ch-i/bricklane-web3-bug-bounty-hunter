# Don't compare boolean expressions to boolean literals

_Section severity (from Solodit section header): Informational_  
_Audit firm: AuditOne_  
_Source report: [2022-10-19-Newwit.md](https://github.com/solodit/solodit_content/blob/main/reports/AuditOne/2022-10-19-Newwit.md)_

---

**Description:** 

Comparing to a constant (true or false)is a bit more expensive than directly checking the returned boolean value.

**Recommendations:** 

Use `require(!paused,"Mint Paused!")`instead of `require(paused == false,"Mint Paused!")`.
