# Omit SafeMath usage

_Section severity (from Solodit section header): Informational_  
_Audit firm: Zokyo_  
_Source report: [2021-08-19-RUSD.md](https://github.com/solodit/solodit_content/blob/main/reports/Zokyo/2021-08-19-RUSD.md)_

---

**Description**

Since Solidity 0.8.x has built-in support of overflow/underflow restrictions, SafeMath lib can be
safely omitted. Such solution will decrease gas usage of the contract.

**Recommendation**:

Omit SafeMath
