# Lack of events in the implementation of privileged functions

_Section severity (from Solodit section header): Informational_  
_Audit firm: Zokyo_  
_Source report: [2023-11-30-CVI.md](https://github.com/solodit/solodit_content/blob/main/reports/Zokyo/2023-11-30-CVI.md)_

**Severity**: Informational

**Status**: Resolved

**Description**
