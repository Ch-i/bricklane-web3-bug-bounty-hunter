# A Model-Driven Automation Pipeline for Building Operation

_arXiv ID: 2611.21631_  
_Authors: Elias Kohler, Sascha Hammes, Michael Vierhauser, Philipp Zech_  
_Published: 2026-09-15_  
_Note: Accepted for publication at the 27th International Conference on Product-Focused Software Process Improvement (PROFES 2026)_  
_Source: [https://arxiv.org/abs/2611.21631v1](https://arxiv.org/abs/2611.21631v1)_

## Abstract

Building Control Systems (BCS) engineering in the Architecture, Engineering, Construction & Operations (AECO) domain remains a complex, manual, and error-prone process, with fractured tools and manual commissioning causing design and built reality to diverge into a performance gap. We argue that a fully integrated and automated, tool-supported workflow spanning design, programming, and operation is feasible and can improve efficiency while reducing manual effort and configuration errors. Building on model-driven engineering, we present an automated process pipeline for modeling, programming, and auto-commissioning BCS. It uses two domain-specific modeling languages with tooling support to synthesize and deploy executable artifacts from platform-independent models, replacing a fractured, multi-stakeholder handoff process with a defined four-step workflow and improving consistency across the engineering lifecycle. Our evaluation reports notable efficiency gains, improved adaptability, moderate-to-high user acceptance, and good performance with solid scalability.
