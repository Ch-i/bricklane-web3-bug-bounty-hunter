# iGRAIL: Blockchain-Based Generative Federated Learning for Trustworthy Healthcare Intelligence

_Published: 2026-11-13_

## Abstract

Healthcare organizations collect huge amounts of patient data from clinical records, medical tests, monitoring systems, and healthcare services. Such data can underpin intelligent healthcare analytics but remains distributed across institutions and cannot be shared directly due to privacy, security, and trust concerns. Federated learning (FL) enables collaborative learning without sharing raw patient records, but the exchanged model information remains vulnerable to privacy leakage, tampering, replay, and unauthorized access. In this paper, we propose iGRAIL, a blockchain-enabled generative FL framework for trustworthy healthcare intelligence. iGRAIL combines local generative learning for data augmentation with differential privacy to protect sensitive information during local training. Model communication is secured using cryptographic mechanisms, and blockchain-based verification and smart-contract access control ensure that only authorized, authentic, fresh, and integrity-preserving updates are accepted for federated aggregation. The framework is evaluated on four healthcare datasets and compared with the conventional FL and blockchain-based FL (BC-FL) under predictive, privacy, non-IID, scalability, malicious-update, security-verification, and runtime settings. For the two large diabetes datasets, iGRAIL improves accuracy by about 5.4-5.6%, the F1-score by 14.6-16.9%, and MCC by 16.0-18.1% over the best FL or BC-FL baseline. Increasing the strength of differential privacy reduces the privacy-loss parameter $ε$ by about 72.5% for the cancer dataset and 87.5% for the diabetes dataset, with only a limited loss in prediction performance. These results indicate that iGRAIL supports secure and scalable federated computing while preserving privacy, model integrity, and controlled participation, at the cost of additional computational overhead.
