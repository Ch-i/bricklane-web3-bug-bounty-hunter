# GI-RTL-Fuzz: Grammar-Informed RTL Generation with LLMs for End-to-End Differential Simulator Fuzzing

_arXiv ID: 2610.38489_  
_Authors: Ziyue Zheng, Zhi Qu, Yangdi Lyu_  
_Published: 2026-10-28_  
_Source: [https://arxiv.org/abs/2610.38489v1](https://arxiv.org/abs/2610.38489v1)_

---

## Abstract

Register-Transfer Level (RTL) simulators are fundamental to modern IC design workflows, yet like any complex software, they frequently contain critical bugs that compromise simulation accuracy. To systematically stress-test these tools, we introduce GI-RTL-Fuzz, a grammar-informed fuzzing framework that leverages Large Language Models (LLMs) to generate high-quality RTL test cases. Our key innovation is guiding the LLM's generation process using Backus-Naur Form (BNF) grammar coverage information, enabling the exploration of complex and rarely-tested language features. By performing differential testing against different RTL simulators, our framework uncovered a significant number of previously unknown inconsistencies in both the linting process and simulation results of major Verilog simulators.
