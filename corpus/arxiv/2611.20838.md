# Optimal Dynamic Fees for L2s: A Vault Approach

_arXiv ID: 2611.20838_  
_Authors: Ulysse Pavloff, Lin Oshitani_  
_Published: 2026-11-16_  
_Note: 24 pages, 3 figures, 3 tables_  
_Source: [https://arxiv.org/abs/2611.20838v1](https://arxiv.org/abs/2611.20838v1)_

## Abstract

Layer 2 (L2) blockchains incur costs when posting data to Layer 1 (L1) and charge users fees intended to recover those costs. We model this mechanism as a *vault*: user fees increase its balance, L1 posting costs reduce it, and surplus and deficit accumulate over time. Forecast-based fees aim to recover posting costs, but forecast errors accumulate in the vault. Returning the vault balance to a target requires feedback on the accumulated error. Treating the vault as a discrete-time control system, we derive in closed form the optimal trade-off between vault tracking and fee smoothness in an idealized benchmark. The optimal rule retains a fraction of the previous fee and corrects in proportion to the current vault error. This fee-memory fraction is the single parameter tracing the entire trade-off frontier. We compare proportional (P), proportional-integral (PI), and Arbitrum-style controllers against this frontier, both analytically and on historical Ethereum fee data. At the fee movement implied by Arbitrum's current parameters, the optimal rule reduces the benchmark tracking error by about one third. Fee memory improves the tracking-smoothness trade-off.
