# fx(hash): Salvaging The Source Code of 28k Generative Artworks

_arXiv ID: 2611.29604_

_Published: 2026-11-22_

## Abstract

fx(hash), the most radically inclusive generative art NFT platform, went bankrupt and shut down on August 17, 2026. The ownership of its 2,705,350 artworks is immutable on the Tezos and Ethereum blockchains, but the source code that generates them is off-chain and in peril. We salvaged all 28,193 generative programs contributed by 5,532 international artists and archived them in Software Heritage. Here we explain why NFT generative art is fragile and the steps we took to salvage these software cultural landmarks.
